Dhokla Recipe




Description

Dhokla is a soft, fluffy, and savory Gujarati snack made from gram flour (besan). It is steamed until light and spongy and is usually served with green chutney. It is a popular Indian breakfast or evening snack and can be enjoyed warm or at room temperature.

Ingredients

For the Dhokla

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • ½ cup water
  • ½ cup curd
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-green chilli paste
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Eno fruit salt
  • Oil for greasing

For the Tempering

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

For Garnishing

  • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Grated coconut (optional)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix gram flour, curd, water, ginger-green chilli paste, turmeric powder, sugar, and salt to make a smooth batter.
  2. Let the batter rest for about 15–20 minutes.
  3. Grease a steaming plate or tray with a little oil.
  4. Add lemon juice and Eno fruit salt to the batter and gently mix. The batter will become light and fluffy.
  5. Immediately pour the batter into the greased tray.
  6. Steam the dhokla for about 15–20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  7. Remove the tray and let the dhokla cool for a few minutes.
  8. For the tempering, heat oil in a small pan and add mustard seeds.
  9. Once they start spluttering, add sesame seeds, curry leaves, and green chillies.
  10. Add water and sugar and bring the mixture to a quick boil.
  11. Pour the tempering evenly over the steamed dhokla.
  12. Cut the dhokla into square or diamond-shaped pieces and serve with green chutney.
